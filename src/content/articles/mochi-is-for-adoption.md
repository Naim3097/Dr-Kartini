*Mochi is four months old in August 2025.*

**9 August 2025.** While taking a stroll, I heard a soft "meow" from a busy street in Shah Alam. It raised a lot of curiosity, but for this "Mummy Cat-Tini", curiosity led to a rescue job.

Mochi had lost her way, and probably her parents too. How sad to be an abandoned child in a world that is so strange and harsh. Cars were honking at her and she was totally confused on her journey to look for a home.

Mochi had to be rescued immediately from the harsh reality of the human environment. I didn't need to call 999, as I was naturally the Mummy Cat-Tini springing into action.

## Can you give Mochi a home?

Mochi is now looking for a safe home where she can be placed and loved for the rest of her life. If you are interested in being a papa or mummy to baby Mochi, please contact "Mummy Cat-Tini" on WhatsApp at [+60 11-2616 6480](https://wa.me/601126166480).

Are you thinking hard about adopting her?

## Before you adopt

Adopting a rescued animal is a lifelong commitment. Before you reach out, think about:

- Whether everyone in your home agrees to having a cat
- Time for daily care, play and companionship
- The cost of food, vaccination, neutering and vet visits
- Keeping her safely indoors, away from traffic
